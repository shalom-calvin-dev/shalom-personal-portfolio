TECHNOLOGY ICON ASSETS - SOURCE, LICENCE AND PERMISSION STATUS
========================================================

How the icons are used
----------------------
The website shows transparent PNG files from public/brand-icons/. Each PNG is an unedited
rasterisation (256 x 256, original proportions) of an SVG source, made by a local helper script that is
not part of the repository. Converting an SVG to PNG does not change the licence or trademark status of
the artwork. Eight PNGs are rendered from ORIGINAL artwork in design/brand-icons/original/ (rtmp,
authentication, third-party-api, excel, microsoft-graph-api, office-365-connectors,
azure-identity, azure-storage). The other PNGs are rendered, without any edit, from the SVG files
listed below. No SVG file is stored in public/ (so none is served by the website). The SVG sources of the other
PNGs, and eight supplied files whose origin is unverified or whose artwork did not match, are kept
unmodified in a local, git-ignored archive (reference/icon-archive/) that is not part of the repository. No artwork
was redrawn, simplified or recoloured. Trademarks belong to their owners; this site is not
affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Microsoft.

Status codes used below
-----------------------
  A  Microsoft's own artwork: byte-identical to a file in Microsoft's official icon downloads
     (A* = official file, but Microsoft gives it a different name than the technology shown).
  B  Redistributed by a third party that cites Microsoft's own icon guidance as the source.
  C  Matches a community mirror (MS Icons) only; not found in Microsoft's official downloads.
  D  Supplied by the owner; origin could NOT be verified. Licence and permission unconfirmed.
  E  Unknown licence.

  3. Power Fx (status B) is licensed "custom" by reference to Microsoft's guidance; the CC0 licence of

     licence, which is not a Microsoft permission.
  5. The original artwork has no formally determined licence or ownership.
  6. The eight archived originals (status A*, D and E above) are not in public/ and not in the
     repository, so the website does not serve them. Their licence and origin concerns remain if you ever
